Detailed Description

Signal value plot panel.

Shows how selected numeric/signal fields change over time. The left pane lists all SIG:* fields discovered in the loaded events; the user checks/unchecks signals to add or remove their line series from the chart on the right. Any numeric field (not just SIG:*) can also be plotted by pressing the "Show all fields" toggle.

Refresh() is called lazily by MainWindow when the tab becomes visible.
