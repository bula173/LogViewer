Makes action show/hide the tab that holds page.

◆ RemoveTrackedTabs()

void ui::qt::utils::RemoveTrackedTabs ( QTabWidget *  tabs,
std::map< std::string, QWidget * > &  tracked 
)
inline

Removes every page listed in tracked (id -> page) from tabs, schedules it for deletion and clears tracked.

Pages are looked up by widget, so tabs that are not tracked (the built-in Events, Statistics, ... tabs) stay untouched wherever they sit, even after the tabs were reordered.
